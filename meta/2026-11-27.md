## three.js viewer (session "three")
Summary: the Sunset & Silver Lake 3D viewer is built and live at https://curb.here/sunset/. Notes: [three/three.md](../three/three.md) (model, controls, hosting) and [three/three-photos.md](../three/three-photos.md) (field photos).
- **Data:** `scripts/osm_to_scene.py` bakes OSM into `docs/sunset/data/scene.json` (288 buildings, roads, sidewalks, crossings, kerbs, 145 KB). Terrain is interpolated from the building `ele` values: overpass.ch elevation is a 30 m surface model outside Switzerland, so it includes roofs. The browser never calls Overpass.
- **Viewer** (`docs/sunset/`, three.js 0.170.0 with no build step): orbit and street view; WASD, Q/E, R/F, Space jump, Right Shift or `/` to strafe, wheel zoom; presets; shareable URL-hash views; buttons hidden by default with Show buttons and a reset/reload button; touchscreens get orbit only.
- **Field photos:** 10 Mapillary photos (2016-01-30 to 2023-06-20), shown flat or projected onto the surfaces, with numbered per-photo checkboxes, first-wins precedence, a "Photos in view" time range and vantage views 10 ft behind each camera position. Only photos 1 and 2 were checked in the viewer; 3–10 were picked by metadata. A single photo looks right from its vantage and smears away from it (limits in three-photos.md).
- **Hosting:** GitHub Pages serves `main:/docs`, and the Worker proxies `curb.here/*` to it. The zone's 4 h browser cache had been serving stale files, so `scripts/stamp_sunset.py` adds a content-hash `?v=` to the page's files and the Worker sets its own cache headers (`worker/`, deployed with wrangler 3.99.0, version c06ae378).
- **Rules added:** `AGENTS.md` now says several agents work at once, and that new photos aren't verified unless asked.
- **Decisions:** `site/` was replaced by `docs/sunset/` to match Pages, which resolves the note in the section below. The tag `sunset-photo-1` marks the one-photo state (`b86323e`).
- **Open:** touchscreen walk mode, curb ramps, junction surfaces, road widths from sidewalks, turn arrows, bridge model, flat-photo jump parallax ([plan/plan.md](../plan/plan.md)), a wrangler update. The full list is in `three/three.md`, Steps.
